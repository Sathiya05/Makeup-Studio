Healing

Know What to Expect

Brows heal in stages — here is the honest timeline before your touch-up.

  1. Days 1–2

    Fresh & Defined

    Colour looks bold and shape crisp. Mild tenderness is normal — keep the area clean and dry.

  2. Days 3–7

    Soft Flaking

    Light flaking as skin renews. Never pick — let it shed naturally and follow your kit routine.

  3. Weeks 2–4

    True Colour Blooms

    Tone softens up to 40% into its healed shade. Patience now pays off in natural results.

  4. Weeks 6–12

    Perfecting Touch-Up

    We refine shape and even out tone for a long-lasting, balanced finish.
