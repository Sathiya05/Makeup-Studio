Aftercare Instructions

First 24 Hours

Keep the area clean, avoid touching, rest and stay hydrated.

Days 2–7

Gentle cleansing, thin aftercare balm if advised, no picking.

After One Week

Colour softens; avoid sun, exfoliants and retinoids nearby.

What to Avoid

Excessive moisture, saunas, swimming, scratching flakes.

When to contact the studio: unusual swelling, spreading redness or concerns — always follow your artist's specific instructions.
